# Support Policy

Support requests should be submitted through the GitHub repository
associated with the Appoena Datadog Agent.

## Support Channels

Support for the software is preferably provided through GitHub Issues, any other requests can be submitted through email to `suporte@appoena.io`

## Supported Topics

- Installation assistance
- Configuration guidance
- Bug reports
- Compatibility issues
- Operational errors

## Supported Classification

Issues must use the predefined repository labels.

Issues that do not follow the labeling policy or misuse labels may be
closed or reclassified by repository maintainers.

## Response Targets

Critical Issue: 1 business day
High Priority: 2 business days
Normal Issue: 5 business days

## Unsupported Items

- Custom feature development
- Environment-specific scripting
- Third-party platform issues
- Direct support outside the repository

Improperly submitted issues that do not follow the templates may be
closed without response.
